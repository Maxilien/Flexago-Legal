Flexago Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Flexago (“we”, “our”, “us”) provides a logistics marketplace that connects Senders and Travelers for delivery services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information, including mobile phone numbers used for SMS communication.

Information We Collect

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery and travel details
  • Verification information
  • Device and usage data

How We Use Your Phone Number

Your mobile number is used for:

  • Account verification
  • Security alerts
  • Job updates
  • Delivery confirmations
  • Support messages

Mobile Number Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers with third parties. Mobile numbers are used exclusively for Flexago service communication.

SMS Consent

By creating an account or texting START to our number, you consent to receive SMS messages from Flexago.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on account activity.

Message & Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out Instructions

You may opt out at any time by texting STOP. You may request help by texting HELP.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.
